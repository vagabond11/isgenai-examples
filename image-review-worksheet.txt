READING THE RESULT
An AI label records a statement about a file. Editable details can be changed.
A signature check concerns the signed record; it does not verify a depicted event.
No signs found does not certify a camera photograph. Saved details can be removed.
Use the original file and supporting sources when the picture's meaning matters.
